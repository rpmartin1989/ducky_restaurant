HOW IT STARTED

The best meals have
always felt like home.

Ducky began with a simple idea: create the kind of restaurant that feels familiar before you even sit down.

The food is inspired by Sunday dinners, handwritten family recipes, cast-iron skillets, crowded tables, and the dishes people keep coming back for generation after generation.

We wanted Ducky to feel less like a restaurant and more like being invited into somebody's home — only with a few more biscuits on the table.

WHAT WE BELIEVE
